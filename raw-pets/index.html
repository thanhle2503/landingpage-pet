Raw feeding basics

General guidance to read before you switch. Your vet knows your pet; ask them first.

Switch slowly

Mix a little raw into the old food and build up over a week or two. A sudden switch is the usual cause of an upset stomach.

Complete meal or a part?

Complete meals are made to be fed alone. Single cuts, organs and many grinds are parts of a homemade diet, often built as about 80% meat, 10% bone and 10% organ, with half of the organ as liver.

Bones: raw only

Never feed cooked bones; they can splinter. Choose a size your pet can't swallow whole and stay nearby while they chew.

Handle it like raw meat

Thaw in the fridge, keep thawed food cold, and use it within the days the label gives. Wash bowls, boards and hands after each meal.

Weigh and adjust

The amounts here are starting points. Weigh your pet every couple of weeks and feed a little more or less to keep a healthy shape.

Check with your vet

Talk to your vet before changing the diet of a puppy, kitten, senior, pregnant pet or any pet with a health condition, and before starting a supplement.
