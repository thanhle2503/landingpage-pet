Pet.Picks notes: which food fits which dog

How we would choose, based on what each brand publishes.

New to fresh food: start with a JustFoodForDogs fresh meal mixed into the old food, and build up over a week or two.

Raw feeders: Raw Pets complete raw meals are made to be fed alone; single cuts and grinds are parts of a homemade diet.

Picky eaters: a small freeze-dried pack is a cheap way to test a new protein before buying a big bag.

Puppies, seniors and dogs with health issues: check with your vet before changing the diet.

Questions, answered

Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

Is fresh dog food better than kibble?

Fresh and raw food use less processing than kibble, and many dogs eat it more eagerly. It costs more per day. Your vet can tell you what suits your dog.

How do I switch my dog to fresh or raw food?

Mix a little of the new food into the old food and increase it over 7 to 14 days. A sudden switch is the usual cause of an upset stomach.
