Pet.Picks notes: which supplement fits which pet

How we would choose, based on what each brand publishes.

Older dogs that are slowing down: look at joint chews with glucosamine and chondroitin from either brand.

Itchy skin or a dull coat: an omega oil or a skin and coat chew is the usual first step.

Upset tummy: probiotic or pumpkin powders can be sprinkled on food.

Before you start: ask your vet, especially for puppies, pregnant pets or pets on medicine.

Questions, answered

Short answers based on what each brand lists on its product pages.

Do dogs need supplements?

A dog on a complete diet may not need any. Supplements are used for a specific need, such as stiff joints or itchy skin. Ask your vet first.

Chews or powder: which is easier?

Soft chews work like a treat for most dogs. Powders mix into food and suit picky eaters or cats.
